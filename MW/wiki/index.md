## Analysis — the evidence trail
_Every adopted and rejected strategy change, dated. This is the validation record._

**Adopted**
- [[structural-exit-test-2026-07-25]] — the 50-day-SMA trend-hold beat the prior swing exit in all three years (+$230k vs +$32k). **Adopted.**
- [[profit-protection-2026-07-25]] — the +30%→+15% profit lock added +$89k over 3 years and won every year; lower locks, give-back trails and partial sales all tested worse. **Adopted.**
- [[process-improvements-2026-07-09]] — the discipline layer v2.1: regime-gated order count, extension cap, cooldown, position/group caps. **Adopted.**
- [[stop-target-optimization-2026-07-02]] — the prior (v2) swing stop/target work, superseded by the trend-hold.

**Rejected — and why**
- [[benchmark-underperformance-2026-07-26]] — pyramiding add-ons: +$101k over 3 years but lost 2025; six refined variants all failed the all-years gate. **Rejected.** Also root-causes index underperformance (cash drag by design; the book beats equal-weight).
- [[flex-exit-backtest-2026-07-25]] — regime-conditioned exits: strong in-sample 2026, failed 2025 out-of-sample. **Rejected as overfit.**
- [[books-revalidation-2026-07-27]] — re-read the trading canon and ranked candidate adjustments; the volume-confirmation filter was then tested and **rejected** (hurt all three years).
