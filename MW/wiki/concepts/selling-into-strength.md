# Selling into strength

**Definition:** Trimming a position in increments *while it's rising* — rather than waiting for the
"perfect" top — to bank profit and reduce risk. The exit-side complement to [[position-scaling]].

## How it works
- When the stock shows strength, sell a **small portion (~20–25% of the current position)** — "a
  little cream off the top," never the whole thing.
- **When:** stock extended from a breakout; hitting new highs; significantly outpacing the market.

## Why it works
- **Guarantees realized profit** — money in the bank.
- **Reduces psychological stress** — once some profit is secured you're "playing with house money."
- **Keeps you in the game** — retaining most shares means you still benefit if it keeps climbing, and
  you've already locked gains if it pulls back.

## Trade-off
- You may "leave money on the table" if it keeps running — accepted in exchange for **consistency and
  longevity** ("singles and doubles, not home runs").

## Open question / debate
- Sits in tension with pure **let-winners-run** trend-following. Track sources on both sides; the
  resolution here is partial trimming, not full exit. See [[position-scaling]].
