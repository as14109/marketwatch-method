# Market breadth

**Definition:** How widely a market move is shared across stocks/sectors. Broad participation (small
caps, many sectors rising) signals a healthier rally than a narrow, mega-cap-only advance.

## Current state
- **2026-06-24 — concentrated selloff, broad market resilient.** A **semiconductor crash (PHLX −7.9%)**
  dragged the Nasdaq −2.2%, but the **S&P 500 Equal-Weight fell only −0.4%** and 6 sectors rose — classic
  "bad index, ok breadth." Defensive rotation (staples/health/utilities/REITs); VIX +12% to 19.43. — [[2026-06-24-key-list]]
- **2026-06-22 — re-broadening; Fed dip bought.** The 06-18 session reversed most of the FOMC selloff
  (Nasdaq +1.9%, info tech +2.7%); participation widened — **Russell 2000 +2.1%**, MidCap +1.1%,
  homebuilders +3.6%, mega-cap growth reasserted. Leadership (semis/AI) intact. — [[2026-06-22-key-list]]
- **2026-06-18 — narrowing / distribution.** A [[kevin-warsh]] higher-for-longer FOMC turned the tape
  risk-off: S&P −1.2%, all 11 sectors red, **all 7 Magnificent Seven lower**, distribution volume.
  Industrials/financials/semis held up best. Rates up (2y 4.16% / 10y 4.46%). — [[2026-06-18-key-list]]

> [!warning] Contradiction — breadth flipped in 3 days
> **2026-06-15** read was **broadening / risk-on** (small+mid caps, materials, financials, utilities,
> REITs joining; Russell 2000 +0.8%; materials +1.8%; falling oil; calmer rates 2y 4.09%/10y 4.49%).
> **2026-06-18** is **narrowing / distribution / risk-off** after the Fed. Newer supersedes for the
> current tape; the open question is whether it's a one-day shakeout or a leadership reset.

## Why it matters
- Broadening breadth widens the opportunity set beyond semis and supports staying constructive; a
  reversal back to narrow leadership would be an early warning. Watch as a regime signal in [[overview]].
