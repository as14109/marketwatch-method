# Eight Keys to Unlocking Superperformance

**Definition:** [[mark-minervini]]'s summary framework — four keys to **generate big returns** plus four
to **limit drawdowns** — protecting the two assets that matter: **capital and confidence**.

## Four Keys to Generating Big Performance
1. **Timing** — you *can* time entries along the "line of least resistance" (the [[vcp]] pivot); seek
   "velocity trades" (20–50% in weeks) to compound fast.
2. **Don't Diversify** — concentrate in 4–12 best names; most money in the top 4–5 ([[position-scaling]]).
3. **Turnover Is Not Taboo** — with an edge, turnover is good; "date stocks, don't marry them"; rotate
   to where capital works best, even to cash.
4. **Always Maintain the Risk/Reward Relationship** — every trade an intelligent risk/reward decision ([[expectancy]]).

## Four Keys to Limiting Drawdowns
1. **Sell Into Strength** — sell into eager buyers while up 20–40%; it's better to sell early than late
   ([[selling-into-strength]]). Time value: twelve 10% gains = +214%.
2. **Trade Small Before You Trade Big** — build exposure *on success*; banked profits "finance" bigger
   risk; never revenge-trade. Beware the **Three Deadly Trader Traps: Emotions, Opinions, Ego.**
3. **Always Trade Directionally** — never buy a falling stock; essential when using tight stops.
4. **Protect Your Breakeven Point** — once you have a gain, move the stop to breakeven; never give it back.
