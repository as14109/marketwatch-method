# Harmonic patterns

**Definition:** A family of **Fibonacci-ratio reversal patterns** (popularized for the wiki by
[[suri-duddella]]) that define a precise entry, stop, and target where a multi-leg price swing completes
at specific Fibonacci retracement/extension ratios. A rule-based **responsive / mean-reversion** technique.

## The main patterns (XABCD swings)
- **ABCD** — the base measured-move building block.
- **Gartley** — B retraces ~0.618 of XA; D completes ~0.786 of XA.
- **Bat** — D completes ~0.886 of XA (deeper, tighter stop).
- **Butterfly** — D extends to ~1.27 of XA (overshoots X).
- **Crab** — D extends to ~1.618 of XA (the deepest extension).
*(Exact ratios per the pattern; the point is each leg must hit defined Fibonacci levels to qualify.)*

## Why it matters / how it connects
- Entries are **at a Fibonacci cluster, against the immediate move** → a formal version of buying
  **below value** ([[auction-market-theory]] responsive activity), the counterpoint to momentum breakouts.
- The completion zone is strongest when it's **cross-verified** by MAs / prior S/R ([[cross-verification]]).
- Distinct from the **continuation/reversal** structures in [[chart-patterns]]; Fibonacci levels are the
  shared tool.

## Caution
- Pattern-fitting is subjective and prone to hindsight bias; treat as one input, size to risk
  ([[key-list-trade-rules]]). Built on intraday/FX/futures as much as equities.
