# VCP — Volatility Contraction Pattern

**Definition:** [[mark-minervini]]'s signature base pattern: a price consolidation that shows
**progressively smaller pullbacks** (volatility contracting from left to right) accompanied by a
**dry-up in volume** — signalling that supply has been absorbed and the stock is ready to advance.

## What to look for
- A series of contractions (e.g. a 25% pullback, then ~15%, then ~8%) — each shallower than the last.
- **Volume drying up** at the right side of the base (the "footprint" of supply exhaustion).
- A precise **pivot / entry point** at the top of the final contraction — the low-risk buy trigger
  used in [[sepa]]. A break back into the base = a [[key-list-trade-rules]]-style violation.

## Why it matters
- Gives an objective, low-risk **entry pivot** with a logical stop just below the last contraction —
  exactly the "constructive base / basing near highs" language the Key List uses (see
  [[2026-06-15-key-list]]).
