# The 7-Bells

**Definition:** [[alan-farley]]'s seven original setups for locating outstanding short-term opportunities,
each tied to a phase of the [[pattern-cycle]] and graded **reward:risk-first** before entry.

## The seven
1. **Dip Trip** — price moving against a strong trend rebounds sharply → **buy the pullback** (responsive). Apps: 38-62, Continuation Dip, First Pullback.
2. **Coiled Spring** — constricted price (contraction) gives way to a directional move → **volatility-contraction breakout** (≈ [[vcp]]). Apps: Triangle Coil, Failure Coil I/II.
3. **Finger Finder** — candles flag a reversal in the next-smaller timeframe. Apps: Hidden 100, 2B Finger, Death Star.
4. **Hole-in-the-Wall** — gap-down after a strong rally signals a trend change → **short**. Apps: 1-2 Decline, Support Hole, Island Reversal.
5. **Power Spike** — high-volume events print the future direction of price. Apps: Breakdown / Pullback / Climax Spike.
6. **Bear Hug** — weak markets drop quickly after rallying into resistance → **short**. Apps: 50-200 Pinball, Channel Short, Intraday Short.
7. **3rd Watch** — breakout through a triple top signals a major uptrend → **breakout buy** (initiating). Apps: Watch Echo, 3rd Watch Classic, Swing Watch.

Plus two classics he adapts: the **2B Top** (Sperandeo) and the **Channel Break**.

## How it connects to our process
- **Dip Trip / First Pullback** = the Key List add trigger "pullback to the 10/21-day MA then bounce."
- **3rd Watch / Coiled Spring** = our [[pyramid-50-30-20-strategy]] base-breakout adds; Coiled Spring ≈ [[vcp]].
- All graded by reward:risk → [[key-list-trade-rules]] / [[expectancy]]; best when S/R lines up ([[cross-verification]]).
