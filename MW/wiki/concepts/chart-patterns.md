# Chart patterns

**Definition:** Recurring price-structure formations used to anticipate the next move. The shared
vocabulary across the wiki's technical sources; a **hub** page linking specific patterns to the phase of
the [[pattern-cycle]] they belong to.

## Continuation (trend pauses, then resumes)
- **Triangles** (ascending = bullish, descending = bearish, symmetrical), **flags**, **pennants**,
  **wedges**, **rectangles/channels**, **cup-with-handle** — all are **contraction → breakout** structures,
  i.e. cousins of [[vcp]] and Farley's **Coiled Spring** ([[seven-bells]]).

## Reversal (trend ends)
- **Head & shoulders** (+ inverted), **double/triple tops & bottoms** (M/W), **rounded tops/bottoms**,
  **diamonds**, **V-tops/bottoms**, **2B / 1-2-3** (Sperandeo). Tops flag the [[pattern-cycle]] topping
  phase → exits / [[selling-into-strength]].

## Measuring & context tools
- **Gaps**, **Fibonacci retracements/extensions & fans**, **channels** (Donchian, linear-regression,
  broadening/megaphone), **Andrew's Pitchfork**. For Fibonacci-ratio reversal patterns see [[harmonic-patterns]].

## How to trade one (per [[timothy-knight]])
- **Identify → define the trigger (breakout/level) → pre-place the stop just beyond the pattern → manage
  reward:risk.** Highest odds when the pattern's level is **cross-verified** ([[cross-verification]]).
