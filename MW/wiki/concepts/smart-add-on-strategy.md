# SMART add-on strategy

**Definition:** A **defensive** [[position-scaling]] method for choppy markets that lack a clean
uptrend. You start small and add in increments sized as a **percentage of your current holding**, so
the position grows but never reaches full intended size.

## SMART acronym
- **S** — Simple chart patterns
- **M** — Manage risk
- **A** — Analyze the market
- **R** — Realize profits by selling into strength ([[selling-into-strength]])
- **T** — Trade safe

## How it works
- **Initial entry:** 20–35% of your ideal full position.
- **Add-ons:** a % of what you *currently* hold — not of the ideal size.
- **Worked example (ideal 1,000 sh):** start 250 (25%) → add 20% of 250 = 50 → 300 →
  add 20% of 300 = 60 → 360. Asymptotically approaches, never reaches, 1,000.
- **Exit:** sell into strength (trim ~20–25%) — see [[selling-into-strength]].

## When to use
- Volatile / uncertain markets with no solid upward trend. Defensive mode: "get on base, not swing
  for the fences." Goal is consistency and capital preservation, not maximum upside.

## Trade-offs
- **Pro:** low risk, low stress, stays in the game across regimes.
- **Con:** caps upside — by design you never reach full size, so you leave money on the table in
  strong trends (where [[pyramid-50-30-20-strategy]] fits better).
