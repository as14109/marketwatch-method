# SEPA — Specific Entry Point Analysis

**Definition:** [[mark-minervini]]'s trading methodology (from *Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard*) for
finding **superperformance growth stocks** and timing precise, low-risk entries. A momentum/growth
cousin of [[can-slim]].

## The five pillars (Minervini's framework)
1. **Trend** — the stock must be in a confirmed uptrend (see [[trend-template]]).
2. **Fundamentals** — strong, accelerating earnings & sales, expanding margins, a catalyst.
3. **Catalyst** — a reason institutions will keep buying (new product, demand driver).
4. **Entry point** — buy at a precise low-risk pivot, typically out of a [[vcp]] base.
5. **Exit** — predefined stop and profit-management rules (small losses, never give back big gains).

## Why it matters
- It's the disciplined, risk-first version of growth-stock trading the wiki keeps circling: select
  leaders, buy a tight pivot, keep risk small, let winners run. Operationalized for us in
  [[key-list-trade-rules]] and [[position-scaling]].
