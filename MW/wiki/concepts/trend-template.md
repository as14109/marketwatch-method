# Trend Template

**Definition:** [[mark-minervini]]'s **non-negotiable qualifier** — an 8-point checklist a stock must
*fully* pass to be considered in a confirmed **Stage 2 uptrend**. It's the first cut in [[sepa]]: fail
any criterion and the stock is off the radar.

## The 8 criteria (from [[think-and-trade-like-a-champion-minervini]])
1. Price is **above** both the **150-day** and **200-day** moving averages.
2. The **150-day MA is above the 200-day MA**.
3. The **200-day MA is trending up** for at least 1 month (preferably 4–5+).
4. The **50-day MA is above both** the 150- and 200-day MAs.
5. Price is **≥25% above** its 52-week low (the best names are often 100–300%+ above).
6. Price is **within 25% of** its 52-week high (closer to a new high is better).
7. **Relative Strength (RS) rank ≥70**, preferably **80–90s**; RS line in an uptrend (≥6–13 weeks).
8. Price is **above the 50-day MA** as it emerges from a base, ideally on a **volume pickup**
   (institutional support).

## Why it matters
- Forces you to **buy only confirmed leaders in healthy uptrends** — the "M" (market/trend) discipline
  of [[can-slim]], made mechanical. Maps directly to the Key List's "basing near highs / elite RS" names.
