# Trading psychology & discipline

**Definition:** The mental-game principles common to the trader-interview and psychology canon —
Schwager's *Market Wizards* (incl. Van Tharp), Lefèvre's *Reminiscences of a Stock Operator* (Livermore),
Koppel & Abell's *The Inner Game of Trading*. Synthesized for our process; not a text reproduction.

## Recurring lessons
- **Risk management is the edge, not prediction.** The Wizards agree win rate matters less than making more
  when right than you lose when wrong ([[expectancy]]). Cut losses fast; there is always another trade.
- **Follow the system; don't override it.** Most failure is behavioral — fear/greed causing traders to
  abandon their rules at the worst moment. A **mechanical** process is protection against your own emotions.
- **Sit with winners.** Livermore: "it was never my thinking that made the big money, it was my sitting" —
  the discipline to hold a trending winner is rarer and more valuable than good entries.
- **Position sizing / survival.** Never risk enough on one trade to be knocked out; size so a string of
  losses is survivable (Tharp's position-sizing emphasis).
- **Self-awareness.** Know your own biases; the enemy is internal (Koppel/Abell).

## How it maps to our system
- Our fully **mechanical** book of record ([[gated_book]], [[key-list-trade-rules]]) *is* the "follow the
  system" discipline — no discretionary overrides. That is a feature, directly endorsed by this canon.
- **"Sit with winners"** is exactly what exit v3 (trend-hold, no fixed target) enforces; the old +12%
  target was the behavioral mistake ("take the quick profit") that [[drift-review-2026-07-25]] measured
  costing 7–15 points.
- **Fixed fractional sizing** ($100k full / 0.86% risk at the disaster stop) + the **regime gate** and
  **heat caps** are the "survive to catch the big trend" rules.
- **Implication:** the biggest psychological risk now is **not trusting the trend-hold through a pullback**
  (selling a winner early by hand) or **overriding the regime gate** to force trades in a downtape. The
  system is built to prevent both — the discipline is to let it run.
