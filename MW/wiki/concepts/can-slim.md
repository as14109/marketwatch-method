# CAN SLIM

**Definition:** William O'Neil / *Investor's Business Daily* (IBD) growth-investing methodology — buy
fundamentally strong leaders breaking out of sound bases in a confirmed up market. The Mission Winners
[[2026-06-15-key-list]] uses its vocabulary and ratings throughout.

## The acronym
- **C** — Current quarterly earnings (strong, accelerating)
- **A** — Annual earnings growth
- **N** — New product/service/management or new price high
- **S** — Supply & demand (volume; share float)
- **L** — Leader, not laggard (high relative strength)
- **I** — Institutional sponsorship (accumulation)
- **M** — Market direction (only buy in a confirmed uptrend)

## IBD ratings glossary (as they appear in the Key List)
- **Composite Rating** (1–99): blended score; 99 = best (e.g. [[ftnt]] = 99).
- **RS (Relative Strength)**: price performance vs. the market; "elite RS" = top-tier.
- **EPS Rank**: earnings strength score.
- **Accumulation/Distribution** (A–E): institutional buying pressure; **A+** = heavy accumulation.
- **Group/Industry Rank**: strength of the stock's peer group (e.g. "top-ranked group").
