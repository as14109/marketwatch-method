# Market Profile

**Definition:** [[peter-steidlmayer]]'s method of organizing a session's price action into a **distribution
(bell curve)** to reveal **value** — where the most trade occurred — and the market's structure. The
graphical, statistical view of [[auction-market-theory]].

## Building blocks
- **TPO (Time Price Opportunity):** divide the session into time brackets (letters A, B, C…); mark each
  price that traded in each bracket. Stacked, the letters form a distribution for the day.
- **[[value-area]]:** the price range covering the **first standard deviation of volume (~70%)** — the
  "fair" zone where most business was done.
- **Point of control (POC):** the price with the most TPOs/volume — the distribution's peak / fairest price.
- **Initial balance:** the range of the first hour; **range extension** beyond it signals
  longer-timeframe participants entering.
- **Excess:** fast, unanswered moves that mark extremes and become durable reference points (support/resistance).

## How to read it (relative to value)
- **Responsive activity** — buying **below** value / selling **above** value (fading away-from-value prices).
- **Initiating activity** — buying **above** value / selling **below** value (pressing the move).
- A balanced (normal) profile ⇒ rotational/two-sided; an elongated profile ⇒ trending/one-timeframe.

- Philosophy: [[auction-market-theory]]; key reference: [[value-area]]; inventor: [[peter-steidlmayer]].
