# J. Peter Steidlmayer

**What it is:** Veteran Chicago Board of Trade (CBOT) member and trader; **inventor of Market Profile**
and the auction-market framework for reading price.

## Snapshot
- Developed [[market-profile]] at the CBOT (introduced publicly in the 1980s) as a way to organize the
  "chaos" of pit trading via the **bell curve / [[value-area]]** and **TPO** distributions.
- Core philosophy: the market is a continuous **auction whose purpose is to facilitate trade** — see
  [[auction-market-theory]].

## Method highlights (from [[steidlmayer-on-markets]])
- Reference price against **value**: **responsive** trades buy below / sell above value; **initiating**
  trades press above/below value.
- Read **excess** (fast, unanswered moves) as durable barriers/reference points.
- Emphasis on a **sound background** of market knowledge and patience over fast shortcuts.
