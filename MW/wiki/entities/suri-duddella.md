# Suri Duddella

**What it is:** Trader and author known for **harmonic pattern** trading and market-structure analysis;
author of *Trade Chart Patterns Like the Pros*.

## Snapshot
- Cataloged a wide range of [[chart-patterns]] and popularized rule-based **[[harmonic-patterns]]**
  (Gartley, Bat, Butterfly, Crab) defined by precise **Fibonacci ratios**.
- Uses Fibonacci levels, channels, and Andrew's Pitchfork as measuring/target tools.
