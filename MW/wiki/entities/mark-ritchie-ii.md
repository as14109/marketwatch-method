# Mark Ritchie II

**What it is:** Momentum/growth trader; metrics-driven risk manager. Son of trader Mark Ritchie; manages
capital via RTM2, LLC.

## Snapshot
- Won **Minervini's 2010 Triple-Digit Challenge** (+100% in <6 months); up **540% since 2010** (+110%
  in 2014 alone). Degree in philosophy.

## Method highlights (from [[momentum-masters-minervini]])
- Starts small (**~50 bps** risk per new position) and **scales into success**.
- **Total portfolio risk gated by results:** ~100 bps when struggling → 200–300 bps when working → up to
  500 bps open risk *only after* banking profits. "When things aren't working, I'm not going to take on more risk."
- Stops derived from his **win/loss math** (mid-single-digit %); sells **into strength** above his average win (≥2× risk).
