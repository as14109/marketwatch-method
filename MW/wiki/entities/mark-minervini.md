# Mark Minervini

**What it is:** Champion momentum/growth trader and educator; creator of the **SEPA** methodology and
the **Volatility Contraction Pattern**. Author of *Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard*.

## Snapshot
- ~30+ years trading; averaged **~220%/yr for 5 consecutive years** (≈36,000% total); **1997 US
  Investing Champion** (+155%, long-only stocks). Featured in Schwager's *Stock Market Wizards*.
- Methodology: [[sepa]] (Specific Entry Point Analysis) + [[vcp]] base detection; small/mid-cap leaders.

## Method highlights (from [[momentum-masters-minervini]])
- **Concentrate:** up to 25% per position (optimal-f / Kelly for a 2:1 trader); 4–8 best names.
- **Risk small:** 1.25–2.50% of equity per trade; "uncle point" max stop ~10%, rarely hit.
- **Never give back a big gain:** move to breakeven after a multiple of risk; sell at 2–6× risk and trail.
- **"Violations" checklist** to sell early; **"sell down to the sleeping point."**
