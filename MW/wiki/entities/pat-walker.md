# Pat Walker

**What it is:** Trader/educator presenting for the *Our Mission Winners* YouTube channel; says he has
been trading since **1986**.

## Snapshot
- Teaches discipline- and psychology-focused **[[position-scaling]]** methods.
- Advocates capping initial risk, adding to confirmed winners, and [[selling-into-strength]].
- Uses classic [[can-slim]] / IBD-style technical triggers (base breakouts, 10/21-day MA bounces).

## Methodology contributed
- [[smart-add-on-strategy]] — defensive scaling for choppy markets.
- [[pyramid-50-30-20-strategy]] — aggressive scaling for strong stocks in strong markets.
- Emphasis on protecting "mental capital": "the biggest part of this game isn't what's before your
  eyes, it's what's between your ears."
