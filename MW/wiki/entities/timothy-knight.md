# Timothy Knight

**What it is:** Trader, author, and founder of Prophet.net (charting) and the Slope of Hope blog; author
of *High-Probability Trade Setups*.

## Snapshot
- Teaches **classic [[chart-patterns]]** for real-time trading — a chapter-per-pattern, entry-and-stop
  approach, bullish and bearish.
- Strong emphasis on **defining the stop before entry** and scanning to find patterns in real time.
