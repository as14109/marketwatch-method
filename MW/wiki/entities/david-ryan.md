# David Ryan

**What it is:** Growth-stock trader; protégé of **William O'Neil** and former money manager at William
O'Neil & Co. A purest practitioner of [[can-slim]].

## Snapshot
- **3× US Investing Champion (1985–87)**; combined three-year return **1,379%**. Ran a mutual fund and
  his own hedge fund (Rustic Partners). Featured in Schwager's *Market Wizards*.

## Method highlights (from [[momentum-masters-minervini]])
- **~10 positions at 10%** initial weight; risk **≤1%** of equity per trade.
- **Only adds to winners after new bases** (13% → +5–7% → 18–20%) — this is our [[pyramid-50-30-20-strategy]].
- Sells when the **chart breaks support**; refuses to cap winners (20% gain vs. a 300% run).
- Moves stop toward breakeven after a >5% move.
