# Dan Zanger

**What it is:** Chart-pattern momentum trader; chief technical analyst at chartpattern.com and author of
*The Zanger Report*. Holds a verified world-record short-term return.

## Snapshot
- Turned **$10,775 into ~$18M in 18 months** (≈164,000%) in the late-1990s bull; *Fortune* "My Stocks
  Are Up 10,000%!" Trades **larger/mega-cap** names; comfortable holding overnight/over weekends.

## Method highlights (from [[momentum-masters-minervini]])
- Position count tracks market health: up to ~22 names in strong markets, 5–6 or **all cash** when choppy.
- Risk **1–3%** per trade; caps total portfolio risk ~20%.
- Stops off **rising trendlines** and the **10-day / 21-day MA**; a break → sell or reduce.
- **Reduces in stages** as a stock advances; typically out before earnings.
