# Alan S. Farley

**What it is:** Swing trader and educator; author of *The Master Swing Trader* and the
[[pattern-cycle]] / [[seven-bells]] approach to short-term technical trading.

## Snapshot
- Built a structured short-term system around **repeatable price patterns across timeframes** (the
  [[pattern-cycle]]), **[[cross-verification]]** of support/resistance, and reward:risk-graded setups.
- Bridges momentum and mean-reversion: chases confirmed breakouts (3rd Watch, Coiled Spring) *and* fades
  pullbacks toward value (Dip Trip) — see [[seven-bells]].

## Method highlights (from [[master-swing-trader-farley]])
- **3D charting:** read three timeframes; a setup's meaning depends on its larger/smaller-frame context.
- **Expansion–Contraction & Trend–Range Axis:** know which phase the market is in before acting.
- **Cross-verification:** trade where multiple *independent* S/R methods converge.
- **Reward:Risk first:** define risk and exits before entry.
